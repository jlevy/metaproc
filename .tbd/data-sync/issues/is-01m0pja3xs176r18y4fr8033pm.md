has since been generalized into a tbd guideline, `agent-session-bootstrap`, merged in
jlevy/tbd PR #248 (`c4dd9f38`). Once that ships in a released `get-tbd` and this repo
adopts that release, most of the metaproc doc becomes a duplicate of the guideline.

where the script lives, how `check_supply_chain.py` guards it — and point at
`tbd guidelines agent-session-bootstrap` for the general pattern, the fit test, the
alternatives, and the PATH and pin-drift traps.
