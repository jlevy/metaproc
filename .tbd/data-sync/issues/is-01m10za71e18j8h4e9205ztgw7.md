Phase 3. The 'arch-' prefix named a directory - docs/arch/ - that this plan deletes.

Keeping it groups the seven component references in a fifteen-topic listing, which has real value. Dropping it removes a distinction that may mean nothing to a reader who never saw the directory, and 'arch-file-io' is a worse topic name than 'file-io'.

Whichever way this goes, it must be decided before Phase 5 renames anything else - two topic renames are worse than one.
