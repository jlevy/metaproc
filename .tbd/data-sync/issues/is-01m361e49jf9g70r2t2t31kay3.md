The identity refusal is wired only into run-process (src/metaproc/commands/run_process.py:6082 and :6300 at b2be3447e4). run-step does not call it: with the PR's two-step fixture, declare dataset identity, launch with ds-1, run 'run-step --step record' with ds-2: exit 0, record.txt=ds-2, run-config.yaml still records ds-1; a later resume with ds-1 exits 0 and keeps the mixed outputs. Composite child scopes bypass it too (src/metaproc/models/authored.py:211-214 documents that child declarations are ignored): a child with identity as_of bound through a parent's literal with: binding rewrites its output when the literal changes. Fix: enforce the input-change policy at a shared boundary every execution path crosses (run-process, run-step, run-parallel, composite child scopes after with: resolution) before any task-state or output mutation; reject unsupported forms clearly; cover the same behavioral cases at every entry point.
